Explore Pushkar

Positioned along the revered Parikarma Marg near Badi Puliya, L S Haveli offers convenient access to Pushkar most sacred ghats, ancient temples, and vibrant bazaars.

Moments Away

Pushkar Lake & 52 Ghats

The spiritual epicenter of Pushkar, surrounded by 52 bathing ghats and over 500 temples. Attend the enchanting evening Maha Aarti as golden oil lamps reflect across the sacred waters.

Short Walk

Jagatpita Brahma Temple

One of the very few existing temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma. Crowned with a red spire and goose motif, it dates back to the 14th century and is Pushkar most prominent pilgrimage landmark.

Easily Accessible

Historic Varaha Temple

Pushkar oldest historic temple, built in the 12th century and dedicated to the Varaha avatar of Lord Vishnu. Renowned for its intricate carvings and rich religious heritage.

Nearby

Old Rangji Temple

A rare architectural marvel featuring a dramatic fusion of traditional South Indian Dravidian gopuram and classic Rajput haveli masonry, dedicated to Lord Ranganatha.

Quick Ride / Walk

Pushkar Sunset Point

A beloved viewpoint overlooking the desert sands and the Aravali hills. Watch the sun dip behind the desert horizon amidst rhythmic drumming and evening melodies.

Local Experience

Town Bazaars & Camel Dunes

Stroll through Pushkar vibrant market lanes filled with authentic rose water, handmade leather goods, and silver jewelry, or venture into the Thar dunes for an afternoon safari.
